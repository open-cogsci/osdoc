# How to find Eye Trackers compatible with OpenSesame

To find Eye Trackers that are compatible with OpenSesame:

1. Visit the OpenSesame website (https://osdoc.cogsci.nl/).
2. Look for the "Eye tracking" category in the documentation under Manual.
3. Review the list of Eye Trackers that are currently supported by OpenSesame. This list includes popular brands such as EyeLink, SMI, and Tobii.
4. Check the specific models and versions of each Eye Tracker brand to ensure compatibility with your setup.
5. If your Eye Tracker is not listed, search the OpenSesame forums or contact the developers to inquire about potential support for your specific device.

# How to set up an Eye Tracker in OpenSesame

To set up an Eye Tracker in OpenSesame:

1. Ensure that your eye tracker is supported by OpenSesame.
2. Install the required Eye Tracker drivers and software on your computer, following the manufacturer's instructions.
3. Connect your Eye Tracker to your computer using the appropriate cables or wireless connections.
4. Launch OpenSesame and create a new experiment or open an existing one.
5. Add a `pygaze_init` item to your experiment sequence to initialize the Eye Tracker connection.
6. Configure the `pygaze_init` item by selecting your Eye Tracker brand and model from the dropdown menu and specifying any additional settings (e.g., calibration type, sample rate).
7. Add a `pygaze_calibrate` item to your experiment sequence to perform the necessary calibration procedure for your Eye Tracker.
8. Use `pygaze_start_recording` and `pygaze_stop_recording` items to control when the Eye Tracker starts and stops collecting data during your experiment.
9. You can also start from the "Eye Tracking Template", which already provides the basic structure of an eye tracking experiment.
