# How to navigate the overview area efficiently in a large experiment

To navigate the overview area efficiently in a large experiment:

1. Use the scroll wheel on your mouse to quickly scroll through the items in the overview area.
2. Click and hold the scroll bar's thumb (the draggable part of the scroll bar) to quickly jump to a specific section of the overview area.
3. Use the arrow keys on your keyboard to move the selection up or down one item at a time in the overview area.
4. Press the "Home" key to jump to the top of the overview area, and press the "End" key to jump to the bottom.
5. Collapse items that you are not currently working on by clicking the expand/collapse symbol next to the item name. This will reduce the amount of scrolling needed.

# How to optimize OpenSesame's usability for large experiments

To optimize OpenSesame's performance for large experiments:

1. Break your experiment into smaller, more manageable parts by using linked copies of items and sequences.
2. Use keyboard shortcuts to quickly navigate and edit items in your experiment (e.g., "Ctrl+C" to copy, "Ctrl+V" to paste, "Ctrl+S" to save).

# How to use the search function to find specific items in a large experiment

To use the search function to find specific items in a large experiment:

1. Press "Ctrl+Space" to open the item quick switcher.
2. Type the name or part of the name of the item you are looking for in the search bar.
3. Press "Enter" or click the search button to highlight the first occurrence of the search term in the overview area.

# How to use the item toolbar to quickly add items to your experiment

To use the item toolbar to quickly add items to your experiment:

1. Locate the item toolbar. It is typically on the right side of the OpenSesame window, although the position can be changed.
2. Click and drag the desired item, such as a `keyboard_response` or `sketchpad`, from the item toolbar to the appropriate location in the overview area.
3. Release the mouse button to add the item to your experiment.
4. The properties of the new item will be automatically opened.

# How to use the undo and redo functions to revert changes in your experiment

To use the undo and redo functions to revert changes in your experiment:

1. Make changes to your experiment, such as moving or editing items.
2. Press "Alt+Ctrl+Z" or click the "Undo" button in the main toolbar to revert the last change you made.
3. Press "Alt+Ctrl+Z" multiple times to undo additional changes in the reverse order that you made them.
4. Press "Alt+Ctrl+Shift+Z" or click the "Redo" button in the main toolbar to reapply a change that you previously undid.
5. Press "Alt+Ctrl+Shift+Z" multiple times to redo additional changes in the order that you originally made them.
